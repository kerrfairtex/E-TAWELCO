Why these themes?

Heritage

Formal green, gold, and white suit institutional, school, and civic communication. It feels grounded without borrowing official seals.

Bangsamoro

Green and white lead, with red kept intentionally small and gold reserved for highlights—regional without becoming loud.

Sama-Bajau

Vinta-inspired stripes and warm cream create a tactile, local lens. Saturated colors are separated rather than stacked.

Ocean & Vinta

Navy, sea blue, teal, cream, and gold create a calm contemporary coastal system for public storytelling.

Recommended default: Heritage for a civic, school, or institutional audience—it communicates trust and place first, while remaining warm and distinctly rooted in Bongao.
